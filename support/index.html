Common questions

  • Can Ikat move my money? No. It asks for read access to your accounts through Plaid and has no way to start a transfer.
  • Where is my data? Encrypted on your phone. If you turn on backup, an encrypted copy goes to your own iCloud Drive.
  • I forgot my password. Your recovery key opens your data and lets you set a new password. Ikat can't reset it for you.
  • How do I delete everything? In the app, More, Settings, Delete account removes what our server holds and disconnects your banks at Plaid.
  • A bank isn't syncing. Open More, Banks and tap Sign in again next to it, or pull down on any screen to refresh.
