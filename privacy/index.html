Your money stays yours

Privacy policy for Ikat.

Ikat keeps your finances on your own devices. Its server only helps your phone reach your bank, and it never keeps what your bank says.

What stays on your phone

Your accounts, balances, transactions, holdings, categories, notes, budgets, goals and settings are stored in the app on your device, encrypted, not on our servers.

If you turn on backup, Ikat encrypts a copy with a key only your devices hold and saves it to your own iCloud Drive. We can't open these backups, and neither can Apple.

What our server keeps

  • Your sign-in: the account ID from Sign in with Apple, and the email address Apple shares (Apple can share a private relay address instead).
  • Your devices: each signed-in device's public key and a record of its session.
  • Your bank links: for each bank, Plaid's ID for the link and its access token, encrypted with a key only your devices hold.

Nothing financial: no account names, balances or transactions. Requests are logged by route and result only.

How your bank's data reaches you

Ikat connects to banks through Plaid. You sign in to your bank on Plaid's screens, and Ikat never sees your bank password. When your phone refreshes, the request goes through our server to Plaid and the answer comes back sealed so only that phone can read it. During the beta, the server holds it in memory while it passes it along, and never stores or logs it.

Ikat only reads. It can't move money.

Who else sees anything

  • Plaid, to connect your banks, under its own privacy policy.
  • Apple, for sign-in and as the home of your encrypted backups.
  • A price service receives the ticker symbols of investments you hold (never amounts or anything about you), to look up prices.
  • Amazon Web Services hosts our server.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell or share your data. Alerts are notifications your phone shows itself.

Leaving

Delete your account in Settings: we remove everything our server holds about you and disconnect your banks at Plaid. Deleting the app removes the data on that device; delete the backups from your iCloud Drive to remove those too.

Questions or requests: support@getikat.com. If this policy changes, the app will tell you before the change applies to you.
